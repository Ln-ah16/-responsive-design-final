Colors of the Rainbow

Welcome to the Colors of the Rainbow gallery! This website explores nine beautiful colors and their unique appearances in everyday life. Each image represents a different color and shows how colors make our world more interesting.

Colors can influence the way we see and experience the world. Red, orange, and yellow often remind us of energy and sunshine, while green and blue bring nature to mind. Indigo, purple, pink, and white offer their own distinctive beauty. Explore the gallery and discover the beauty of each color.
